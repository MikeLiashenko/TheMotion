About this journey

One continuous zoom from the observable universe to a carbon atom in the leaf of a tree, and on into the atom’s nucleus. There is a stop at every power of ten or so on the way.

Finding your way

  • Scroll, drag or use the arrow keys to zoom. It works in both directions.
  • → and ← travel to the next and the previous stop; Home and End to either end.
  • The marks on the right-hand edge are the stops: point at one for its name, click it to go there.
  • Point at a label for a line about the thing it names.

What is real here, and what is not

  • The number at the top right is the true height of what the screen shows. Sizes and distances are to scale unless the line beside the scale bar says otherwise.
  • Where things would be far too small to see at their true size (planets among their orbits, galaxies among their neighbours) they are drawn larger, and the page says so at the time.
  • Inside the Milky Way, stars, clusters and nebulae stand where they really are. Further out, distances are roughly right and directions are not.
  • The Earth is real: photographs from space, from the air and of a real meadow on the Rhine. Clouds near the ground are generated.
  • From the tree downwards everything is a model: the leaf, its cells, the DNA and the atom are drawn from what is known of their sizes and shapes, in colours chosen to tell their parts apart. Nothing down there has a colour of its own.
  • The galaxies, nebulae, the Sun’s face and the planets’ surfaces are generated to resemble the real ones. They are impressions, not images.
